Sunday 24th May 2026

The NLP Day

Today has been strange. Not really, though. It has simply been the natural result of feelings that have been building up for a long time. And it isn't finished yet. Recently my dreams have been memorable. I remember them when I wake up, which has never happened before. But my dream maker, I suppose my subconscious, is in touch with my conscious mind. They have the same anxieties. I dream about lack of money, violence in the streets, and other things which are uppermost in my mind. With the result that my days are disturbed. I have little patience and play games with those I meet. This morning I woke at 3 and thought this is it. I'm tired of being useless. It's time to start making real changes. But what? What am I really cut out to do? So I first read a bit of War and Peace. The introduction had my answer. Leo Tolstoy was 34 and had the same feeling. Unlike me he had money and a large estate, but also felt her had achieved nothing. Time to take the bull by the horns. Within a year he found a suitable wife, 16 years his junior, a book to write, and meaning. In this his wife assisted him greatly. She created an environment which made him happy to write all day long, and also rewrote his day's work every night, so it was suitable to be published. This to me is quite something. I have the same weaknesses. I need the right people in my life, and the right environment. To some extent I have that at the moment, But I need more. I need to find the thing that's bothering me. I have a before-and-after life. Before a certain point in my life I had energy, direction and drive. I did not simply float through life, available to other people to use as they like. NLP had the answer. It deals directly with that. That point where things went wrong, and how to put that in its proper place. Instead of that one misstep becoming your identity, the original self is recovered. Instead of wandering about damaged, you can place that slip in its proper place. Of little value in your view of yourself.

That is, you don't have to accept the new identity forced on you by a very unfortunate accident. And the technique is simple. The interesting thing is how your subconscious sits waiting for you to do this. The moment I read the page and a half describing the technique, my mind was ready. I didn't quite understand the steps yet, well enough to do them on myself. I wanted a therapist, and didn't feel like joining the 7-day free trial offered online. I gave Gemini the PDF and asked it to be the world's top NLP practitioner. It was quite easy. First task was, find a point where you were at your peak. This was hard for me because after the 'bad thing' happened, I still kept on fighting it for 5 years or so. So there was a day which was the watershed. Strangely, one where nothing much happened. But I remember it. A day when I was alone and had the thing in my hand which always gave me most pleasure: a model aircraft. A model of my real  life which was going, although I fought very hard to keep it. And in his wisdom Gemini, using the pdf,  took it from there. Simply keep that identity, that feeling of mastery and play, those fragments of your real self, and bring it past the bad times which lay ahead, to today.

And it worked.
