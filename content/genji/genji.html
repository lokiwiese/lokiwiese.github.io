The Tale of Genji

by Murasaki Shikibu

I came across this book in Haruki Murakami's "Kafka On The Shore". In the book Kafka Tamura, the 15-year old protagonist, sees a ghost. He suspects that the 15-year old girl that he saw sitting at his desk is Miss  Saeki, who is still alive, a 50-year old woman for whom he is developing an infatuation. When he asks his friend  Oshima, about it, whether living people can have ghosts, Oshima tells him the story of Genji.

"Now the end has come, and I am filled with sorrow that our ways must part:

the path I would rather take is the one that leads to life."

A must-read for anyone who respects the power of love.
