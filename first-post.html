So here goes 😂. Note: Please read this with your tongue firmly in your cheek. And then, please hear my confession. 1) Ask me if I believe in the things that normal people believe in, like religion, war, money, a job, a car, a family, a house, an education, morality, law, anything conceived by the human monkey on his way up in the world? And the answer is no, or rather, perhaps. I can stand back and analyze these things, without being part of it. I have lent my ear to the cleverest people I could find, in a lifetime of searching, and still my mind scorns most things. Living in South Africa has supported this view. Is this bad? No. I harm no-one, I am happy and joyful, and I even find love. The female of the species is quite willing to procreate. Even at 75. They want babies like me, perhaps with less smelly armpits and with both eyes that open fully. But that gorgeous rebellious brain? That ironic sense of humour? That lean healthy body? They want babies like that. This has given me a feeling of empowerment. Like I'm the new Jesus. I can save the world. If only I wanted to. For me the saved world would have very few humans in it. Me and my offspring. Under my guidance and control. Because the average human means well, but needs guidance. From me, and others like me. Now do you see? 😂 I meant to write a simple letter of apology, and explanation, and it gets out of hand. Why? There's an evil genius lurking in my brain. And he takes over when I'm bored. So beware.
