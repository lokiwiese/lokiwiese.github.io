In Saldanha Bay lies Holon. A 20 foot sloop bought by my daughter. In perfact seaworthy shape. Small enough to single hand. That's my ONLY goal. Besides making my house comfy and filling my plot with fruit trees. And building a blog and writing sea stories. And publishing books about the sea and other things. As I said, my ONLY goal. To sail her and make her happy. A lonely boat is a sad boat.
